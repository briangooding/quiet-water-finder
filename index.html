How it works. It pulls public land from the USGS Protected Areas Database (PAD-US), streams, ponds, marshes and springs from the USGS National Hydrography Dataset, parking and trailheads from OpenStreetMap, and aquatic insect sightings from iNaturalist. Every stretch of water that touches public land becomes a spot.

Hidden score. Higher means less known: unnamed water, far from parking and trailheads, few iNaturalist records nearby, small ponds and seeps where dragonflies, damselflies, mayflies and caddisflies breed.
